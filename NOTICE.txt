BSR–RMT interface utilities

The LS interface, which supported single-configuration targets, was described
by Juan C. del Valle, Aaron T. Bondy, Soumyajit Saha, Kathryn R. Hamilton
and Klaus Bartschat, Atoms 13, 75 (2025).
https://doi.org/10.3390/atoms13090075

Juan C. del Valle created the original interface utilities described in that
paper. Aaron T. Bondy and Juan C. del Valle created the versions of
bsr_Splinewaves.f90, bsr_H_jk.f90 and bsr_d_jk.f90 described in the current
manuscript, “Multiconfigurational and Spin–Orbit Extensions of the BSR–RMT
Interface.” bsr_Splinedata.f90 is unchanged from the original paper.

The BSR programs and associated libraries were created by Oleg Zatsarinny and
collaborators. See Computer Physics Communications 174, 273–356 (2006).
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2005.10.006
